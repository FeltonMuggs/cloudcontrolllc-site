# FoMGL Sponsorship — Lead Generation Content Kit

Government Blockchain Association · Future of Money, Governance & the Law (FoMGL)

Outbound and inbound content for filling the FoMGL sponsorship pipeline. Every
asset here is written to a single job: get a qualified sponsorship conversation
booked with a company that already wants to sell into government.

## Placeholders — fill these before anything ships

No dates, prices, headcounts, or logos are invented in this kit. Every unverified
fact is a bracketed token. Search for `[` across these files and replace from the
official GBA sponsorship prospectus before sending a single message.

| Token | What goes here |
|---|---|
| `[EVENT_DATES]` | Confirmed FoMGL dates, e.g. "March 3–5" |
| `[EVENT_YEAR]` | Conference year |
| `[VENUE_NAME]` | Venue — for a multi-city series use `[DC_VENUES]` / `[NY_VENUES]` |
| `[VENUE_CITY]` | City — for a multi-city series use `[CITY_SCOPE]` |
| `[SERIES_DATES]` | Full span across cities, for the one-line version |

| `[AGENCY_COUNT]` | Number of agencies represented |
| `[COUNTRY_COUNT]` | Number of countries represented |
| `[KEYNOTE_NAMES]` | Confirmed headline speakers |

| `[TIER_1_NAME]` … `[TIER_5_NAME]` | Sponsorship tier names, highest to lowest |
| `[TIER_1_PRICE]` … `[TIER_5_PRICE]` | Tier prices |

**Rule:** if a claim cannot be sourced to the prospectus or a GBA-approved
number, cut the claim. Nothing in outbound should be a guess.

## How the campaign runs

The pipeline has three sources, worked in this order of expected yield:

1. **Past sponsors and exhibitors** — highest close rate, shortest cycle. Renewal
   sequence, worked first so returning sponsors get first pick of inventory.
2. **Existing GBA organizational members** — already paying, already sold on the
   government audience. Sponsorship is an expansion motion, not a new sale.
3. **Web3 Solutions Directory listings** — cold, but pre-qualified by the fact
   that they self-identified as selling blockchain into government. Route these
   through the `gba-lead-qualifier` skill first; sponsorship is one of the three
   tracks it recommends.

Every inbound lead — prospectus download, "tell me more" reply, booth inquiry —
gets qualified against `icp-and-target-list.md` and logged per `crm-workflow.md`.

## Eight-week run-up

> **This assumes an eight-week runway.** For FoMGL 2026 that runway is already
> gone — see `launch-readiness.md` for the compressed sprint that replaces it.

| Week | Focus |
|---|---|
| 8 weeks out | Renewal sequence to past sponsors; top-tier inventory held for them |
| 7 | Member expansion sequence; open general prospectus |
| 6 | Cold sequence wave 1 to directory ICP-A; LinkedIn connect wave |
| 5 | Cold wave 2; first speaker/sponsor announcement post |
| 4 | Call blitz on all openers and prospectus downloads |
| 3 | Mid-tier push; add-on offers to committed sponsors |
| 2 | Scarcity sequence — remaining inventory, named deadline |
| 1 | Last call to warm-but-unclosed; convert misses into next-cycle nurture |

## Metrics to hold the campaign to

Track these per source, not in aggregate — cold and renewal behave nothing alike.

- Reply rate on outbound (positive replies broken out separately)
- Meetings booked per 100 contacts
- Meeting → proposal rate
- Proposal → signed rate, and average days to signature
- Average sponsorship value, and attach rate on add-ons
- Prospectus downloads → booked calls

If cold reply rate runs under target, the problem is almost always targeting or
the first line — not the offer. Fix the list before rewriting the email.
