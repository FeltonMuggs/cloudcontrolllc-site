# FoMGL Sponsorship — ICP, Sourcing & Scoring

Who to put in the sequence, where to find them, and how to decide who gets a call
this week versus a nurture email next quarter.

## The core insight

A FoMGL sponsorship is not bought for brand awareness. It is bought by companies
that need to be in a room with government buyers and cannot get there any other
way. Every message in this kit is written to that need. Anyone who does not have
a government go-to-market motion is not a sponsor prospect — they are a
membership or BMM prospect, and belong in a different track.

## Sponsor ICP segments

### ICP-A — Government-GTM blockchain vendors (primary)

The default sponsor. Sells a blockchain or Web3 solution, targets public sector,
has a funded BD motion.

- Listed in the GBA Web3 Solutions Directory, or an obvious candidate for it
- Sector tags in the high-intent set: Identity, Elections, Procurement, Records
  Management, Healthcare, Finance, Supply Chain, Security, Property Titling
- 10–200 employees, or a government-facing business unit inside a larger firm
- Has raised outside capital, won a government contract, or run an agency pilot
- **Buying trigger:** a named federal, state, or international pursuit in the next
  12 months that a room full of officials would shorten

### ICP-B — Existing GBA organizational members (expansion)

Already paying $5K–$50K/yr. Sponsorship is the next step up the ladder, not a
cold sale.

- Any Small/Standard/Medium/Large organizational member
- Strongest signal: members who have used speaking, PR, or working-group benefits
  — they already treat GBA as a channel
- **Buying trigger:** membership renewal date, or a BMM level upgrade they want
  visibility for

### ICP-C — Past FoMGL and Capitol Hill Showcase sponsors (renewal)

Highest close rate in the entire pipeline. Work these first and give them first
refusal on top-tier inventory.

- Sponsored or exhibited in any prior cycle
- Sent 2+ people, spoke, or hosted a side event — signals real budget
- **Buying trigger:** the same budget line that funded last year is being planned now

### ICP-D — Adjacent and non-obvious buyers (opportunistic)

Not blockchain vendors, but need the same room.

- Law firms and compliance advisories with a digital-assets practice
- Systems integrators and primes scouting subcontract partners
- Financial infrastructure firms tracking CBDC, stablecoin, and payments policy
- Universities and research centers recruiting government partners
- National and sub-national government delegations sponsoring their own pavilion
- Chapter and regional sponsors — LATAM, EMEA, APAC — funding local delegations

### Disqualifiers

Do not sequence. Route to membership or drop.

- Consumer crypto, retail trading, memecoins, or anything with no public-sector story
- Pre-product companies with no funding and no pilot
- Anyone whose actual ask is a speaking slot they will not pay for — that is a
  program conversation, not a sponsorship one
- Firms in active regulatory enforcement; escalate to GBA leadership before contact

## Sourcing plays

| Play | Source | Volume | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Directory sweep | GBA Web3 Solutions Directory | High | Filter to high-intent sector tags; already self-qualified on gov intent |
| Past-sponsor pull | Prior FoMGL / Capitol Hill sponsor lists | Low | Work first; highest yield per contact |
| Member expansion | GBA organizational member roster | Medium | Coordinate with membership so the ask doesn't collide with a renewal |
| BMM roster | BMM-assessed companies, Lvl 3+ | Low | Marquee-tier candidates; pair sponsorship with stage visibility |
| Working group rosters | 50+ GBA working groups | Medium | Companies contributing engineering time have budget and commitment |
| Speaker inbound | FoMGL speaking applicants not selected | Medium | Warmest cold list there is — they raised their hand |
| Competitor-event scrape | Sponsor lists of adjacent gov-tech and digital-asset events | High | Proven willingness to pay for gov audiences |
| Award and grant lists | SBIR/STTR awardees, state innovation grants, GovTech accelerators | Medium | Funded and mandated to commercialize |
| Referral loop | Committed sponsors, chapter presidents, working group chairs | Low | Ask every closed sponsor for two names |

## Scoring rubric

Score each prospect 0–100. Route by band.

| Signal | Points |
|---|---|
| Past FoMGL or Capitol Hill sponsor | +30 |
| Current GBA organizational member | +20 |
| BMM Lvl 3+ | +15 |
| BMM Lvl 1–2 | +8 |
| 2+ high-intent sector tags (Identity, Elections, Procurement, Records, Health, Finance, Supply Chain) | +15 |
| Named agency contract, pilot, or active RFP response | +15 |
| Raised institutional capital in the last 24 months | +10 |
| Dedicated public-sector BD or capture role on staff | +10 |
| Sponsored any comparable gov-tech event in the last 18 months | +10 |
| Executive already engaged with GBA (speaker, WG member, past attendee) | +10 |
| Sub-10 employees with no funding | −15 |
| No public-sector reference anywhere in their materials | −20 |

| Band | Route |
|---|---|
| 70+ — **Tier 1** | Direct call and personal email from `[SENDER_NAME]`. Lead with `[TIER_1_NAME]` / `[TIER_2_NAME]`. Offer inventory hold. |
| 45–69 — **Tier 2** | Full cold sequence + LinkedIn. Lead with `[TIER_3_NAME]`. |
| 25–44 — **Tier 3** | Prospectus email and nurture only. Pitch exhibitor-level and add-ons. |
| <25 | No sequence. Route to membership or BMM per `gba-lead-qualifier`. |

## Research before first touch — five minutes, four fields

Do not personalize on the company's tagline. Personalize on their government
motion. Capture these into the CRM before the first send:

1. **The pursuit** — which agency, program, or jurisdiction are they visibly
   chasing? Source: press releases, contract awards, careers page, LinkedIn posts.
2. **The proof** — one pilot, award, or credential you can name back to them.
3. **The gap** — what does FoMGL put in front of them that they cannot buy
   elsewhere? Usually a specific category of official.
4. **The person** — who owns the budget? Usually CEO or founder under 50 people;
   VP Marketing or VP Public Sector above that. Never route this to a generic
   `info@` if a named person exists.

If you cannot fill field 1 or field 3, the prospect is not ICP. Drop it and move
on — a well-written email to the wrong company still fails.
