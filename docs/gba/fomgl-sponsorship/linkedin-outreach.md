# FoMGL Sponsorship — LinkedIn Outreach

LinkedIn does the work email cannot: reaching founders and VPs who ignore cold
inboxes but read their notifications. Run it alongside the email sequences, not
instead of them.

Log every prospect touched here into JoMoCo using the `linkedin-to-jomoco` skill,
tagged per `crm-workflow.md`. An untracked LinkedIn touch is a lost lead.

## The ladder

Six steps. Never skip to the pitch — the pitch lands only after the profile is
warm.

| Step | Action | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Engage — comment substantively on a recent post | Day 0 |
| 2 | Connection request, no pitch | Day 1 |
| 3 | Thank-you DM, still no pitch | On accept |
| 4 | Value DM — the audience, not the package | +3 days |
| 5 | Soft ask — prospectus or 15 minutes | +7 days |
| 6 | Break-up DM | +14 days |

If they engage at any step, drop the script and have the conversation.

## Connection requests

Keep under 300 characters. No links — LinkedIn suppresses them and they read as
spam.

**Directory-sourced (ICP-A)**
> [FIRST_NAME] — saw [COMPANY] in the GBA Web3 directory under [SECTOR]. I work on
> the government side of GBA and your [SPECIFIC_THING] is the kind of thing our
> agency members ask about. Would like to connect.

**Post-engagement**
> Your post on [TOPIC] matched what we keep hearing from agency people in our
> working groups. I'm with the Government Blockchain Association — connecting.

**Past sponsor / attendee**
> [FIRST_NAME] — [COMPANY] was with us at [PRIOR_EVENT]. Planning FoMGL
> [EVENT_YEAR] now and wanted to reconnect before we open sponsorships.

**Event-triggered**
> Congrats on [FUNDING / AWARD / CONTRACT]. That puts [COMPANY] squarely in the
> conversation our government members are having. Connecting from the GBA side.

## DM ladder

### Step 3 — On accept

> Thanks for connecting, [FIRST_NAME].
>
> No pitch — I mostly wanted to follow what [COMPANY] is doing in [SECTOR]. We run
> the working groups where agency folks argue about exactly this, so it is useful
> context on my end.

### Step 4 — Value DM (+3 days)

> [FIRST_NAME] — a thing that might be useful.
>
> The pattern I see across [SECTOR] vendors: the technology is not the blocker,
> access is. Procurement portals do not put you in front of the person who can
> sponsor a program.
>
> That is most of why FoMGL exists — [EVENT_DATES] in [VENUE_CITY],
> [ATTENDEE_COUNT] attendees, about [GOV_ATTENDEE_PCT] from government across
> [AGENCY_COUNT] agencies.
>
> Happy to send the agenda if the audience is relevant to you.

### Step 5 — Soft ask (+7 days)

> [FIRST_NAME] — sponsors get the deliberate version of that access: [BENEFIT_1],
> [BENEFIT_2], and program placement assigned in commitment order.
>
> Tiers start at `[TIER_5_PRICE]`. Want me to send the prospectus, or is 15
> minutes easier? `[BOOKING_LINK]`

### Step 6 — Break-up (+14 days)

> [FIRST_NAME] — I will leave it here. If FoMGL [EVENT_YEAR] is not the year, no
> issue at all. If it is worth a look later, message me any time and I will pick
> it back up.

## InMail — Tier 1 only

Different shape: name the ask in the first line, because they know it cost you.

>
> [FIRST_NAME], this is a sponsorship inquiry, so I will be direct.
>
> FoMGL is GBA's conference series on money, governance and the law — [EVENT_DATES],
> [VENUE_CITY]. [ATTENDEE_COUNT] attendees, ~[GOV_ATTENDEE_PCT] government,
> [AGENCY_COUNT] agencies, [COUNTRY_COUNT] countries.
>

>
> [SENDER_NAME], [SENDER_TITLE]

## Engagement plays — how the list warms itself

Outbound converts far better into an audience that already recognizes the name.
Spend 20 minutes a day here.

1. **Comment before you connect.** One substantive comment on a target's post,
   the day before the request. Add a fact or a counterpoint; "Great post!" is
   worse than nothing.
2. **Post about the audience, not the event.** What agency buyers are asking for
   in [SECTOR] outperforms any post about the conference itself.
3. **Tag committed sponsors** in announcement posts. Their networks are full of
   your remaining prospects, and being tagged is a benefit sponsors value.
4. **Watch job posts.** A company hiring a "Public Sector Sales Lead" or "Capture
   Manager" has just funded a government motion. Reach out that week.
5. **Work the speaker list.** Every confirmed speaker's company is a sponsor
   prospect, and every speaker's engaged followers are ICP-A.
6. **Mine event hashtags** from adjacent gov-tech and digital-asset conferences.
   Anyone posting from a competing event's floor already buys this category.

## What not to do

- Pitch in the connection request. Kills accept rate and marks the profile.
- Send the same DM to multiple people at one company. They compare notes.
- Automate the sequence. LinkedIn restricts it and the writing shows.
- Ask for an intro before you have given the person anything.
- Chase past the break-up. One clean exit preserves next year.
