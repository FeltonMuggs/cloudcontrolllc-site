# FoMGL Sponsorship — Organic Content & Campaign Calendar

Outbound gets replies from people who already recognize the name. This is how the
name gets recognized. Post from `[SENDER_NAME]`'s personal profile as well as the
GBA company page — the personal profile consistently outperforms.

## The content principle

Post about the problem sponsors have, not about the conference. A post about what
agency buyers actually ask blockchain vendors will out-reach any "Sponsorships
now open!" graphic by an order of magnitude, and it puts the right people in the
comments — where the real prospecting happens.

Ratio: four problem or insight posts for every one promotional post.

## Cross-channel notes

- **Every post ends with one link,** and it is always `[PROSPECTUS_URL]`. One
  destination, so the analytics mean something.
- **Answer every comment** within a few hours. Comment threads on the problem
  posts are where ICP-A self-identifies — check each commenter against the
  scoring rubric and sequence the ones that qualify.
- **Repost sponsor content.** It costs nothing, sponsors notice, and it is the
  cheapest renewal insurance available.
- **Keep the post-event proof material.** Ask each sponsor at the debrief for one
  sentence you may quote. That sentence sells next year's top tier.
