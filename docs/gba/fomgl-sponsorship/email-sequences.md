# FoMGL Sponsorship — Email Sequences

Five sequences, one per pipeline source. Do not mix them. A past sponsor who gets
the cold email will notice, and it costs a renewal.

## Rules that apply to every send

- **One ask per email.** Book a call, or download the prospectus. Never both.
- **Under 120 words** on cold sends. Executives read on phones between meetings.
- **The first line is about them,** not about GBA. If the first line could be sent
  to any company on the list, rewrite it.
- **No invented numbers.** Every stat traces to `[PROSPECTUS_URL]` or is cut.
- **Plain text.** No banners, no logos in the body, one link maximum.
- **Stop the sequence on any reply,** including negative ones. Handle by hand.
- **Send windows:** Tuesday–Thursday, 7–9am in the recipient's timezone.

## Subject line bank

Test in pairs, one variable at a time, minimum 50 sends per arm before calling it.

**Specific and personal** — highest open rates, lowest scale
- [COMPANY]'s [AGENCY] work
- question about [COMPANY]'s public sector plans
- [FIRST_NAME] — [SECTOR] at FoMGL

**Curiosity**
- the room [COMPANY] isn't in yet
- why [SECTOR] vendors miss government buyers
- what's left for [EVENT_YEAR]

**Direct**
- FoMGL [EVENT_YEAR] sponsorship
- sponsoring FoMGL — [COMPANY]
- [EVENT_DATES] in [VENUE_CITY]

**Renewal**
- holding your [TIER_NAME] spot
- releasing the hold [DATE]
- first look at [EVENT_YEAR]

Avoid: anything with "partnership opportunity," "quick question," "touching base,"
or an emoji. Those read as bulk mail to exactly the executives worth reaching.
