The Carbon Broker

Submission demo for the Flare Summer Signal hackathon (DoraHacks), Interoperable Asset Products track. Six brokered-deal scenarios run end-to-end to a settled, line-item profit.

Open screen-by-screen walkthrough deck
How to demo
Open a scenario and tap Run end-to-end to auto-play every stage, or Advance step to narrate. Settled spreads accrue to the treasury card. The Market tab shows the tokenized-credit listings; the architecture card on Home maps the 4-layer stack.
Flare surface area shown
FTSOv2 feeds (FLR, XRP) · FDC JsonApi + Payment attestations · FAssets FXRP settlement · state-connector-proven XRPL payments · escrow + atomic settlement · ERC-1155 tokenized credits · ERC-721 retirement certificate.
Assumptions: broker point of view, wallet pre-connected on Coston2, registry calls mocked at current integration status. Figures illustrative.
