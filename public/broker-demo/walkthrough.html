What judges are looking at
Product
Brokerage dApp that turns fragmented carbon-credit deals into one auditable, profitable flow: source → attest → match → escrow → settle → book P&L.
Target user
Carbon brokers and aggregators (Inherited Sciences™ first), plus the sellers and corporate buyers on either side of each deal.
How it uses Flare
FTSOv2 pricing, FDC JsonApi proofs of Verra / Gold Standard, FDC Payment proofs of XRPL, FAssets FXRP settlement, escrow + atomic swap contracts.
Newly built
Mobile broker app, six end-to-end deal scenarios, tokenized-credit marketplace, and the FXRP cross-chain settlement path.
